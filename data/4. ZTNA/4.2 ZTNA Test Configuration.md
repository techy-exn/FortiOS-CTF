## Step-1: Check ZTNA tag status

Before we browse to the Webserver we just configured. We need to check our Security Posture tag we do this by opening Forticlient from our system tray.

In the Forticlient window we can click on our User `user1` if you do not see any `Security Posture tags` active we suppose you know what to do since our policy defines that the application paint must be running.

Open up the application paint and within the next sync to FortiEMS you will get the `paint-running` tag.

![alt text](/images/ZTNA-paint-running.jpg)

---

## Step-2: Browse to the webserver

Now that we've ensured our `paint-running` tag is active. We can open the webserver located in the Chrome browser in the bookmark section. The bookmark is named `ZTNA-Dashboard`. Click on the bookmark and you should be able to acccess our webserver. **NOTE** you will need to accept the certificate that is provided with clicking on the bookmark.
 
![alt text](/images/ZTNA-working-tag.jpg)

## Step-3: Test with no tag

If all is working fine please feel free to test out more by **removing** the tag by closing the application paint and waiting for the sync to happen with FortiEMS. What message do you get?
