In the new FortiOS 8.0 update Replacement messages and images are supported globally and per VDOM, allowing administrators to tailor message templates separately for each VDOM and improving control in multi-tenant deployments.

The replacement message GUI has been reorganized into two tabs: `Message` and `Image`.

---

Head over to `System > Settings > Replacement messages`

![replacement.jpg](/files/4fe57b90a747897771d4c7373ae03d9b/replacement.jpg)

Replacement message images are available both globally and in individual VDOMs. When a new VDOM is created, a default replacemsg‑image profile is automatically added.

In the image list view, two columns have been added: Scope and In Use.

Scope indicates whether an image belongs to the Global configuration or a specific VDOM.

In Use shows whether the image is currently referenced by any replacement message.

![vdoms.jpg](/files/60c7acf5a83f8a3c34127b37d6c6e603/vdoms.jpg)
