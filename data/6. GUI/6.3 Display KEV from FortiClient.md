FortiOS 8.0 introduces additional endpoint vulnerability context by integrating Known Exploited Vulnerability (KEV) information received from FortiClient.

<div class="defender-container">
<div class="defender-label">Did you know?</div>

A vulnerability being listed as a KEV is particularly important because it indicates that
the vulnerability is known to have been exploited in the wild.

<strong>KEV</strong> — Known Exploited Vulnerability<br>
<strong>CVE</strong> — Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures

</div>

To keep the workshop focused on the new FortiOS 8.0 functionality, this discovery and synchronization process has already been completed for you.<br>
<br>
<strong>Four KEVs are already present on your FortiGate.</strong>

### 1. Open Asset Inventory
Go in your browser to  FortiGate1<br>
On the FortiGate go to `Security Fabric > Asset Identity Center`<br>

![alt text](/images/Security_fabric.jpg)

### 2. View KEV vulnerabilities
![alt text](/images/Security_Fabric-asset.jpg)
Select the  IT-Workstation1 and click `Asset Details`

From the top bar, select `Vulnerabilities`, then locate the vulnerabilities
marked as **KEV**.<br>
<br>
![alt text](/images/Security_Fabric_asset_kev.jpg)

The KEV entries show the affected CVEs and provide the information needed
to determine the appropriate patch or softpatch.

### 3. Understand what FortiOS is telling you

Take a closer look at the vulnerabilities displayed for the workstation.

Notice that FortiGate shows both the <strong>severity</strong> of a vulnerability
and whether it is identified as a <strong>KEV</strong>.<br>

These two indicators provide different information.

<strong>Severity</strong> helps you understand the potential impact of a vulnerability,
while the <strong>KEV</strong> designation adds information about whether exploitation
has actually been observed in the wild.

<div class="info">

<strong>**Think about it:**</strong><br>

Imagine you have hundreds of vulnerabilities across your endpoints.
Some may have a high severity score, while others are also identified as KEVs.

Which information would help your security team decide which vulnerabilities
deserve more immediate attention?

</div>

The important takeaway is that KEV information provides <strong>additional context
for vulnerability prioritization</strong>. It does not mean that FortiGate has
automatically blocked or remediated the vulnerability.
