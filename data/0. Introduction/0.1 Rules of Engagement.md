> **Welcome** to the **FortiOS 8.0 Workshop**.

<br>

**About this workshop:**

This workshop is designed to introduce you to the latest capabilities and improvements available in **FortiOS 8.0**. Through a series of hands-on exercises, you will explore new features, enhanced security capabilities, and operational improvements while gaining practical experience configuring and managing a FortiGate running FortiOS 8.0.

Each challenge focuses on a specific feature or use case, allowing you to learn by doing in a realistic lab environment.

<br>

> **Your objective:** complete each challenge to discover what's new in **FortiOS 8.0** and learn how these features can improve your organization's security posture.

<br>
